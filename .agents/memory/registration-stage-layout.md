---
name: Registration stage layout
description: Durable separation and sizing rules for the Prison Life registration wizard.
---

Stage 1 is reserved for identity, appearance selection, and the full-height character preview. Prisoner type cards belong only to stage 2.

**Why:** Keeping specialization out of the identity screen prevents the first stage from becoming a crowded four-column dashboard and matches the supplied stage-2 reference.

**How to apply:** When changing the registration layout, preserve the three-column desktop structure for stage 1, let it collapse into a vertical flow on mobile, and keep stage 2 type cards full-width within the registration canvas.
